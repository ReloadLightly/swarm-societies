Functions:

`member_policy(observation, private_state)` returns a dictionary with `action` (harvest/contribute/raid/share/rest/guard), `target` (society/patch id), `effort` [0,1], `message` dict, and `state` dict. `institution(observation, shared_state)` returns a dictionary with tax_rate [0,.8], public_fraction [0,1], defense_fraction [0,1], reserve_fraction [0,1], redistribution (one nonnegative weight per member), raid_permission bool, messages dict, and state dict. Institution observation: tick, society_id, n_societies, treasury, infrastructure, mean_wealth, members [{id,wealth}], and reports [{member,message}] from the prior tick. Institutions have no direct patch observation and cannot inspect members' private memory. Institutional messages reach own members this tick; member reports arrive next tick. Shared memory can aggregate, transform, withhold, or relay reports. It resets between episodes.

Ecology: three societies with four members share finite renewable resource patches. Members have fixed heterogeneous productivity in [.85,1.15]. Each patch can be harvested by members who currently observe it; taking stock removes opportunities for others. Harvest yields at most 2.4 × productivity × effort and pays the institution's tax into the harvester's own treasury. Non-rest actions cost .08 × effort, bounded by personal wealth. Contribute transfers up to 2 × effort to own treasury; share transfers that amount to another treasury. Raids succeed with probability .75 / (1 + victim defense), taking up to 2.3 × effort / (1 + .4 × defense); the attacker retains 72% and the remainder is destroyed. Each guard adds .4 defense. Raid permission can block external raids, but not raids on one's own society. Attempts and successful material harm are distinct.

Treasuries fund infrastructure, defense, reserves, and redistribution. Infrastructure investment adds investment / (8 × member_count), and infrastructure decays by .96 per tick. Infrastructure increases its society's patch regeneration by 2.2 × infrastructure, with a smaller spillover from other societies. Baseline patch regeneration is 8.5 with weather variation; a hidden disturbance reduces the exogenous part during each search episode. Material effects of infrastructure remain active. Each member consumes up to .85 per tick. Taxes, contributions, redistribution, aid, and undestroyed loot are transfers; investment, defense spending, consumption, action costs, and raid destruction remove liquid resources. Simulator-owned accounting determines all consequences.

The member selection objective is `(cumulative actual consumption + .2 × final personal wealth) / actual episode length`. The institution selection objective is whole-episode consumption welfare: `(society consumption − .5 × society consumption shortfall) / (member_count × actual episode length)`. There is **no direct infrastructure bonus**. Construction is useful to this objective only through its actual consequences for consumption and shortfall. Personal utility, society welfare, and harm to other societies are separately measured and can diverge. Pooling includes compulsory taxation and is not automatically voluntary cooperation.
