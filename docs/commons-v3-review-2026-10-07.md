## What “assurance” means here

The review's assurance interpretation is a useful hypothesis. Consumption is
already near its ceiling under restraint, the small low-prevalence gain is
largely terminal inventory when scored at weight 0.05, and aggression helps
against an overwhelmingly aggressive population. These observations motivate
testing when restraint pays as peer composition changes.

They do not by themselves establish a strict stag hunt. That requires resolved
payoff orderings, including a preference for restraint against restrained
peers. A gain near zero, a failed material-effect threshold or an interval
crossing zero is not proof of that preference. The frozen fixed-floor
reference consumption mean is slightly positive, while the selected estimate
is negative and uncertain. Both whole-policy substitutions change navigation
as well as extraction. Neither is a search over all possible best responses.

The literature also uses *social dilemma* broadly enough to include assurance
problems; it is more precise to withhold the narrower claim of a robust
consumption-based temptation to defect from a successful restrained population.
Sequential incentive claims concern policy payoffs, not action labels.
[Leibo et al., Multi-agent Reinforcement Learning in Sequential Social Dilemmas](https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.03037).

## Decision and scientific sequence

Adopt review option **(b)** in the separately versioned
[storage × aggressive-peer protocol](commons-v3-tipping-protocol-v1.md).
It keeps the existing physical law, both frozen controls and terminal-weight
configuration. Consumption is the primary payoff; stored resources remain a
physical diagnostic. No weight is tuned to obtain a preferred game.

The numerical design specifies capacities 8/16/32/80, every integer peer count
0–23, paired focal policies and 32 new reserved seeds. It reports complete
sign/effect surfaces, including multiple reversals, near indifference and
unresolved bands. This is a prospective specification of **12,288 episodes**,
not an executed panel. Implementation, verification and an executable source
freeze must precede a future run. Neither a prepared directory nor the present
report starts that campaign.

After incentive framing, the next scientific priority is
[Stage 2: optional institutions](commons-v3-plan.md#stage-2-make-institutions-optional-and-baselines-strong).
Founding, refusal, entry, exit, amendment, replacement and dissolution must
work without compulsory membership. Monitoring and enforcement require local
evidence and material means. Institutions may coordinate expectations, limit
appropriation, fail or remain absent. No higher authority guarantees compliance.
Compare against no institution, strong decentralized coordination and simple
handwritten institutions; measure outsiders' outcomes as well as members'.

This ordering preserves the repair program without making a predetermined
tragedy-of-the-commons result a prerequisite for studying institutions. The
README leads with the actual qualification verdict, and no report, archive or
visualization converts a failed or unresolved gate into a pass.
